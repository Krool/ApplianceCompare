Kitchen Appliance Compare

Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Kitchen Appliance Compare is a static research site run by Krool and hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no ads and no server of its own. This page lists what loading the site sends to other parties and what it keeps in your browser.

Google Analytics

The comparison page loads Google Analytics 4 (gtag.js from www.googletagmanager.com, property G-SXG8M67HPV) on every visit. There is no consent prompt.

  • The site's own code sends no custom events. It does not report which filters, models or comparisons you use.
  • Google's script records the page view and its standard automatic measurements, along with device and browser type, referrer, and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address.
  • It sets first-party cookies on krool.github.io (_ga and _ga_SXG8M67HPV) that hold a random visitor ID. Google's default lifetime for them is two years.
  • Hits go to google-analytics.com and analytics.google.com hosts.

To stop it, block cookies or those hosts in your browser, or use Google's opt-out add-on. The site works the same without it. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Fonts

The comparison page loads its typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Your browser sends Google your IP address and user agent to fetch them. This privacy page and the 404 page load nothing from Google.

Stored in your browser

The site saves your view settings in localStorage under the key cc:state:v1: the open tab, collapsed filter groups, score weights, and each category's filters and search text. This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The same settings also appear in the page address after the #, so a link you copy carries them. Clear the site's data in your browser to remove them.

Hosting

GitHub serves the pages, data files and product images, and logs visitor IP addresses as described in the GitHub privacy statement. We have no access to those logs.

Links to other sites

Source and retailer links open the publisher's or retailer's own site, which has its own policy. The links carry no affiliate or tracking tags from us.

What is not collected

No names, email addresses or payment details. Nothing is sold or shared beyond the services listed above.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything else, this page and its date will change first.
